Before you install

Straight answers

Is Super Water Sort a game with no ads?

For the first seven days there are no forced ads; optional extra-help ads appear only if you request them. After that, forced ads are between puzzles, at least 13 completed levels apart and never before level 23. A one-time Remove Ads purchase makes the game fully ad-free.

Does “Remove Ads” really remove all ads?

Yes. It blocks forced interstitials and bypasses optional reward ads. There is no subscription.

Can the starting puzzles be solved?

Each of the 3,000 starting boards has a verified solution. Your choices can still lead to a dead end; use the free undos or restart to try a different route.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. The puzzles and progress work offline. A connection is needed for store purchases and any ad that you choose or become eligible to see.

Which Super Water Sort app is this?

This is the official game from Lodestone Games, currently listed under Tom Appleton in the stores: Android package com.infinitygames.liquidsortzen on Google Play. There is no iPhone version yet.
